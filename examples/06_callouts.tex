\section{Technical Callouts}

The following environments are used to highlight important
engineering information.

The intrinsic gain of a MOS transistor is fundamentally related
to its transconductance and output resistance
\cite{razavi_design_2017}.

\begin{designobjective}
	
	Achieve a minimum DC gain of \qty{60}{\deci\bel} and a unity-gain
	bandwidth of at least \qty{100}{\mega\hertz}.
	
\end{designobjective}


\begin{assumption}
	
	The ideal model initially neglects transistor parasitic capacitances.
	
\end{assumption}


\begin{designnote}
	
	The ideal architecture is validated before replacing functional
	blocks with transistor-level implementations.
	
\end{designnote}


\begin{keyresult}
	
	The nominal simulation achieves a DC gain of
	\qty{67.2}{\deci\bel} and a phase margin of
	\qty{63.4}{\degree}.
	
\end{keyresult}


\begin{designconstraint}
	
	The circuit must remain functional at a supply voltage of
	\qty{1.0}{\volt}.
	
\end{designconstraint}
