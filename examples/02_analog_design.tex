\section{Analog IC Design}

\subsection{Typography Test}

The nominal output voltage $V_{OUT}$ is \qty{1.0}{\volt}.
The corresponding schematic node is named \signal{vout}.

The bias transistor \device{M12} carries a nominal current of
\qty{2.5}{\micro\ampere}.

\mutedtext{This value corresponds to the nominal TT operating point
	at \qty{27}{\degreeCelsius}.}

The target gain is 60 dB and the target bandwidth is 100 MHz.
