\section{Equations and Units}
\label{sec:equations_units}
This section demonstrates the mathematical notation used throughout
the design reports.

The target supply voltage is \SI{1.8}{\volt}, while the nominal bias
current is \SI{2.5}{\micro\ampere}.

The target gain is \SI{60}{\deci\bel} and the target unity-gain
bandwidth is \SI{100}{\mega\hertz}.

\subsection{Intrinsic Gain}

The intrinsic gain of a MOS transistor can be approximated by

\begin{equation}
	A_v = g_m r_o
	\label{eq:intrinsic_gain}
\end{equation}

As shown in Equation~\ref{eq:intrinsic_gain}, increasing either
transconductance or output resistance increases the intrinsic gain.

\subsection{Transconductance Efficiency}

A useful design quantity is the transconductance efficiency

\begin{equation}
	\frac{g_m}{I_D}
	\label{eq:gm_id}
\end{equation}

which is widely used when selecting an inversion level.
